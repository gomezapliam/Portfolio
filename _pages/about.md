Liam A. Gómez-Pedraza is a Puerto Rican transdisciplinary artist, choreographer, and performance researcher. He is the founder and Artistic Director of Laboratorio Preliminal *(from Latin* limen*, threshold)*, a transdisciplinary choreographic research space based in San Juan. He holds an MA in Cultural Agency and Administration from the University of Puerto Rico, Río Piedras, where he was a Mellon MAGAC Graduate Research Fellow.

His research develops a theoretical framework he calls *extractive worldmaking*, grounded in Caribbean decolonial performance genealogies and drawing on the work of Diana Taylor, José Esteban Muñoz, and Arturo Escobar. His practice moves across live performance, choreography, video and projection, and interactive installation, and his work has been presented through three Laboratorio Preliminal productions, *Preliminal* (2023), *ABISMAL* (2025), and *Ritual* (2026).

From September 2026 he will pursue an MPhil/PhD in Creative Practice (Collaborative Arts) at Trinity Laban Conservatoire of Music and Dance, London, supervised by Prof. Jonathan Owen Clark and Dr. Naomi Lefebvre Sell. He works multilingually across Spanish, English, Russian, and Italian.
